## 🎨 **Visual Style Guide**

### **Color Palette**
- **Primary Blue**: #3B82F6 (buttons, highlights, primary elements)
- **Accent Blue**: #60A5FA (secondary highlights, hover states)
- **Background Dark**: #0F1E33 (main background, professional tone)
- **Success Green**: #10B981 (validation, positive indicators)
- **Warning Amber**: #F59E0B (attention, medium priority)
- **Error Red**: #EF4444 (negative indicators, high priority)

### **Typography**
- **Main Font**: Inter (clean, professional, highly readable)
- **Title Weight**: 700-800 (bold, authoritative)
- **Body Weight**: 400-500 (readable, approachable)
- **Technical Weight**: 600 (emphasis without overwhelming)

### **Animation Principles**
- **Timing**: 0.3s for interactions, 0.6s for transitions
- **Easing**: Ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits
- **Consistency**: Use same animation patterns throughout
- **Performance**: Optimize for smooth 30fps playback

---

## 🎤 **Audio Production Guide**

### **Voiceover Direction**
- **Tone**: Professional, confident, enthusiastic but controlled
- **Pace**: 150-160 words per minute (clear articulation)
- **Emphasis**: Highlight key technical terms and benefits
- **Breathing**: Natural pauses at punctuation marks
- **Energy**: Start strong, build during demo, close powerfully

### **Recording Specifications**
- **Format**: WAV 48kHz 24-bit (professional standard)
- **Environment**: Treated acoustic space or quality USB microphone
- **Processing**: Light compression, EQ, noise gate
- **Delivery**: Separate files for each scene for easier editing

### **Background Music**
- **Style**: Corporate, upbeat, modern electronic
- **Tempo**: 120-130 BPM (energetic but not overwhelming)
- **Instrumentation**: Digital, clean, professional
- **Licensing**: Royalty-free or Creative Commons
- **Volume**: 25-30% during narration, 50-60% during music-only sections

## 🛠 **Technical Production Notes**

### **Screen Recording Setup**
```bash
# OBS Studio Settings Recommendation
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- FPS: 60 (for smooth cursor movement)
- Bitrate: 50,000 Kbps (high quality)
- Encoder: Hardware (NVENC/AMD) if available

```

### **Post-Production Workflow**
1. **Rough Cut**: Assemble all clips in timeline
2. **Audio Sync**: Align voiceover with visual beats
3. **Color Grading**: Apply consistent look across all clips
4. **Motion Graphics**: Add titles, callouts, and annotations
5. **Audio Mix**: Balance voice, music, and sound effects
6. **Final Export**: H.264, 1920x1080, 30fps, 10 Mbps
